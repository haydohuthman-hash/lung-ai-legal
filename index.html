Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Lung AI creates an anonymous account for you automatically, with no email or password, and backs up your progress to it.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile → Reset everything. It permanently deletes your account and all your data on our server, and clears the app on your phone. To have voice-call recordings deleted, email us.

How do I report or block someone?

In Community, tap the ••• on any post and choose Report post or Block. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.

What is the call button?

It starts a voice conversation with the Lung AI coach, an AI that can help you through a craving. It is not a person and not an emergency service. It needs your microphone, and it asks your permission before your first call.

Is Lung AI medical advice?

No. It gives general information and encouragement. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about quitting aids and medication.

I got a new phone. Can I get my progress back?

Not yet. Because accounts are anonymous, they are tied to the phone they were created on. We are working on a way to move your progress between phones.
