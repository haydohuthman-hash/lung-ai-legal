Common questions

Do I need an account?

You do not need to create a login. When cloud services are available, Patch creates an anonymous account automatically, with no email or password. Your profile, check-ins and earned patches are saved on this device, and the app shows whether your account backup has completed. An anonymous account cannot be recovered after its session is lost.

Which habits can I work on?

You can select smoking, vaping, cannabis, alcohol, scrolling, gambling, pornography or food habits, or name another habit. Food support focuses on regular nourishment and support, not stopping eating or weight loss. Patch offers check-ins, encouragement and support for your own goals. It does not diagnose an addiction or provide treatment.

Where are check-in and Profile?

Tap the plus button beside the bottom navigation to open Check in, your voice notes, sharing and Profile. Text and Call sit directly below Patch on Home; Help with an urge opens reminders, guided meditation and offline ambient sounds.

What happens at check-in?

Choose your battery percentage, optionally add a feeling and intention, then review what you want to share. Creating a daily story sends the disclosed answers to ElevenLabs after saving your check-in. If the story service is unavailable, your check-in stays saved and you can try again. Generated stories stay on this device.

How do Apple Health steps work?

On iPhone, choose to connect Apple Health to show today's steps. Access is optional and read-only. You can set your own daily goal; the suggested 10,000 steps is adjustable and is not a medical recommendation. Your step totals remain on your device and are not sent to the coach or shared with your journey card.

What happens to my patches after a setback?

Your collection records actions such as checking in, reflecting, taking a reset and reaching out. Each earned patch has an earning date and stays on your profile after a setback, a streak restart or clearing your coach chat. Deleting your account and data removes the collection too.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile → Delete my account and data. It permanently deletes your account and all your data on our server, and clears the app on your phone (except the count of today's free calls, which starts again the next day). To have voice-call recordings deleted too, email us the Support ID shown in Profile → Help and about before you delete your account.

How do I report or block someone?

In Community, tap the ••• on any post and choose Report post or Block. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.

What is the call button?

It starts a voice conversation with the Patch coach, an AI that can help you through a craving. It is not a person and not an emergency service. It needs your microphone, and it asks your permission before your first call.

Can I change how the coach sounds?

Yes. In Profile → Your coach you can pick its personality, how it talks to you, and its voice, including Arc Mode's direct, composed voice. You can also withdraw your permission for calls there; we will ask again before your next one.

How many calls can I make?

Call access depends on the plans available in the app. When a paid plan is offered, voice coaching requires an active Patch Plus subscription. While no paid plan is available, the app allows 2 free calls a day on each device, each up to 10 minutes, with the allowance restarting at local midnight. The 60-second reset remains free and works offline. Available plans and current prices appear before purchase; existing Apple subscription records may still use the previous Lung AI name.

Is Patch medical advice?

No. It gives general information and encouragement, and earned patches are not measures of medical recovery. Talk to a health professional about treatment, withdrawal or medication.

I got a new phone. Can I get my progress back?

Not through a recovery login in this version. The anonymous account depends on the session stored on your device. Reinstalling, clearing browser or app storage, or losing the phone can remove access to that session and its backup, including your patch collection. Do not rely on a cloud backup as a way to move an anonymous account to a new phone.
