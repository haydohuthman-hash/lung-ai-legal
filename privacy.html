Patch

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Last updated 15 September 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an email address or password. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically.
  • Your selected habits, check-ins and earned patches are saved on your device and backed up to your private account when connected. The app shows when backup is waiting.
  • Anonymous accounts cannot be recovered after a reinstall or loss of their stored session, even if a server backup exists.
  • The community shows other people only your first name and what you choose to post.
  • Voice calls are handled by our voice-AI provider, ElevenLabs. We ask your permission before your first call.
  • If you join the waitlist on our website, we use your email only to tell you about Patch’s launch. You can leave the list at any time.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete everything, on your phone and on our server, at any time: Profile → Delete my account and data.

1. Who we are

Patch is an app that supports adults in changing habits, including smoking, vaping, cannabis, alcohol, scrolling, gambling, pornography, food habits and a habit they name themselves. Food support focuses on regular nourishment and support, not stopping eating or weight loss. It is provided by Hayden Huthman, an individual based in Queensland, Australia (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at haydenbanksy@icloud.com.

This policy explains what information Patch collects, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and it also sets out the rights available to people in the European Union and the United Kingdom.

2. What we collect, and why

Information you give us during setup

Your chosen name (only its first word is shown to other community users), your age, the habits you select, an optional custom habit name, how often and how much you use (as a range), roughly how much you spend each week when applicable (as a range), your reasons for changing the habit, and the date you started. We use these details to personalise your plan and show progress and applicable spending estimates. We do not treat selecting a habit as a diagnosis.

Your daily logs

Whether you explicitly confirm following your goals that day, any setbacks you record (and which habits they concerned), urges you report getting through, when you checked in, and — if you add them — your battery percentage, mood, reflection or note, and selected triggers. A reflection-only check-in does not confirm that you followed your goals. We use these records for your calendar, check-in history and progress. They are stored on your device and in your private account when backed up. Your settings are stored with your profile: sound, the coach personality, style and voice you picked, and whether you have agreed to our terms, to voice calls and to the community rules.

Your daily challenges

We store the three challenge identifiers you choose for each day, when the selection changed, whether you have ticked them off, and the time each tick or untick changed. An unfinished selection can also be kept in this device's session storage while you browse. Saved choices are part of your private daily history, stored on your device and backed up when the required service is available. Completing a challenge does not automatically confirm following your goals or completing a daily check-in.

Progress you choose to share

You can create a public link showing your live journey timer and a quote you select. The link includes your exact journey start timestamp and a quote identifier so the timer can keep counting in another browser; it does not include your name, habits, account identifier or private check-ins. Images capture a still snapshot of the timer at the moment you choose. Earlier links containing a day number remain still snapshots. Anyone with a link can view, copy or reshare it without an account. The public page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which handles requests under its own privacy policy. A shared link or an exported image can remain available after you delete your Patch account because it is separate from that account. We do not automatically post it to your social accounts.

Your earned patches

We store each earned patch's identifier and earning date on your device and on your private profile when backed up. Patches recognise actions such as starting your plan, checking in, reflecting, completing a reset, contacting the coach and returning after a setback. Your collection stays with your profile after setbacks, streak restarts and clearing text chat; deleting your account and data removes it. Patches do not certify abstinence or medical recovery and are not published to the community automatically.

Community content

If you use the community: what you post (up to 280 characters), the optional streak badge you attach to it, likes you give, posts you report, and people you block. Your posts are shown to other users with your first name only. Other people can see how many likes a post has, but never who liked it. Reports are seen only by us, and a post reported by several people is hidden until we review it. Posts and names are checked against a filter for objectionable words before they are published. Blocks are private to you.

Voice calls with Patch

If you choose to start a call, the app uses your microphone for the length of the call and sends your voice to ElevenLabs, which turns it into text, generates the coach's replies, and speaks them back. To make the coach relevant to you, we also send your first name, the days in your current goal streak, the number of urges you have reported getting through, and the coach personality, style and voice you picked. Your saved reflections, triggers and patch collection are not automatically included in this call context, but anything you say during a call is sent as part of it. ElevenLabs keeps the audio and a transcript of each call in our ElevenLabs account, tagged with your Support ID whenever your account is connected, until they are deleted; you can ask us to delete recordings at any time (see section 8). We ask for your permission before your first call, and you can withdraw it at any time with Profile → Voice calls; we will ask again before your next call. Microphone capture begins only when you choose to start a call or record a private voice note.

Your daily AI stories

When you choose Create my daily story at the end of the check-in, Patch saves your check-in and sends your first name, selected habit identifiers, whether you explicitly confirmed following your goals, that day's battery percentage, and any feelings, triggers, reflection and intention you supplied to ElevenLabs in a text-only session. The request also includes that check-in's date and your selected coaching mode. The AI uses these answers to write an imagined story. This does not use your microphone. Apple Health step totals, other days' check-ins and your chat history are not included. The latest 90 daily stories are saved on this device and are not included in profile backup. Updating a day's story replaces the local copy for that day. Deleting your account and data clears local stories. ElevenLabs processes the submitted text and generated story under its service settings and may retain a conversation transcript; clearing local data does not remove provider records. Contact us about deletion of provider-held data and include the approximate date and time of the story request so we can help identify it.

Your personal plans and toolkit

Your per-habit triggers, planned next moves, chosen support, daily intentions, learning progress, private lesson reflections and guided-pause progress stay on this device. They are not included in your account backup, shared cards, or automatically sent to the coach or story service. Your onboarding plan is assembled on this device from the habits and starting frequency you choose; it is not a medical assessment or a request to an AI service. Deleting your account and data clears this local information.

Your private voice notes

If you choose to record a voice note, Patch captures up to five minutes of audio and the date and time recording began. You can listen back, add an optional title, save it on this device, or discard it. Saved audio, titles, dates and durations are stored in this device's browser/app storage (IndexedDB); they are not uploaded, transcribed, sent to ElevenLabs or included in your account backup. Unsaved notes stay in memory for the current app session. Recordings stop when you leave the recorder or background the app. You can delete individual notes in Voice notes; deleting your account and data also removes saved local recordings. Clearing app/browser storage, reinstalling or losing the device can lose these notes.

Subscriptions

Where a Patch Plus subscription is offered in the iPhone app, Apple handles payment: we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app asks Apple, on your phone, whether your subscription is active, and attaches your Support ID to a purchase when available so it can be matched to your account if you contact us. Existing Apple subscription records may still use the previous Lung AI name. The count of your free calls each day stays on your phone.

Technical information

An anonymous account ID, created when the app first connects to our server. Your profile and posts are stored under it. The app never asks for an email address or phone number; if you email us, we keep your email with your request. The app shows it as your Support ID in Profile → Help and about. Like any online service, our providers also process technical information such as your IP address and device type in order to deliver the service and keep it secure.

Text coaching

The built-in text coach processes messages on your device. If an online text-coaching service is enabled for your app version, your recent messages and replies, chosen name, selected habit identifiers, goal streak, reported urges and applicable spending estimate are sent through our coaching endpoint to Anthropic to generate a reply. Saved reflections, triggers and your patch collection are not automatically added to that context. Your local text-chat history is not part of the Supabase profile backup. Clearing that history does not remove patches earned by reaching out.

The website waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect your email address, when you joined, whether you joined on an iPhone, an Android phone or a computer, and, if you came through a friend’s link, which link it was. We use your email only to tell you when Patch is on the App Store or, if you joined on an Android phone, whether it comes to Android. We use a friend’s link only to show that friend how many people joined through it, never who. You must be 18 or over to join.

Your browser keeps a random code so it can show you your place in line when you come back. It isn’t a cookie, and it is sent only to our database. To stop the form being misused, we keep a one-way scrambled (salted and hashed) form of your IP address for up to one day.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app's features: check-ins, progress, earned patches, coaching and the community.
  • To back up your private profile and logs while your anonymous account session remains available. This does not provide account recovery after reinstalling or losing that session.
  • To keep the community safe: enforcing our rules, reviewing reports, and limiting abuse.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

4. Your habit and health information

Optional Apple Health steps: if you connect Apple Health in the iPhone app, Patch asks only to read your step count. We use it to display today's total and progress toward the step goal you choose. Patch does not write to Apple Health. Step totals and any totals you enter manually are stored separately on this device; they are not uploaded to our server, shared with the AI coach, included in sharing cards, sold, or used for advertising. You can disconnect step refresh in Patch, manage read access in the Health app, and remove Patch's saved step totals through Profile → Delete my account and data. Removing Patch's saved totals does not delete the original records in Apple Health.

Your selected or custom habits, substance use, urges, reflections and triggers can include sensitive personal or health information. Information about your smoking, vaping and cravings may be health information under Australian law, and data concerning health under the GDPR. We collect it to provide the app to you, with your consent. Your private profile and logs are readable only by your own account through the app and are not shown to other users. Information you choose to post or discuss with a coach is handled as described above.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run Patch, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and live-update service, which also holds the website waitlist. Your data is stored in Supabase's Sydney, Australia region.
  • ElevenLabs — provides AI voice calls and daily text stories, as described above. ElevenLabs may process and store call data outside Australia, including in the United States.
  • Anthropic — processes the limited conversation context described above when online text coaching is enabled. That processing may take place outside Australia.
  • Apple — which distributes the app and handles any available subscription payments. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your use of the App Store and to purchases.
  • Other Patch users — who see your community posts and first name, as described above.

When you use the share feature to send something to WhatsApp, Messages, X, Telegram, Reddit or email, you are sending it yourself, and that service's privacy policy applies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's life or safety.

6. Overseas disclosure

Your core backed-up profile and log data is stored in Australia. Voice calls and daily story requests are processed by ElevenLabs, which may handle that data in the United States or other countries. If online text coaching is enabled, Anthropic may process that conversation context outside Australia too. We choose providers that protect data with measures comparable to Australian law, including encryption in transit.

7. How we protect it

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our database uses row-level security, so your profile and logs can be read only by your own account — not by other users, and not by anyone without your account's session. Your data is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

8. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information until you delete it. Earned patch identifiers and dates stay on your profile after a setback, a streak restart or clearing chat, until you delete your account and data.

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Profile → Delete my account and data. This permanently deletes your account and everything on our server — profile, selected and custom habits, earned patch collection, daily logs including reflections and triggers, posts, likes, reports and blocks — and clears the app on your phone, except the count of today's free calls, which starts again by itself the next day. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete a post: tap the ••• on your post and choose Delete.
  • Leave the waitlist: use “Leave the waitlist” on our website, the leave link in any email we send you about the waitlist, or email us. We delete the waitlist once we no longer need it to tell people about launches.
  • Call recordings: email us your Support ID (Profile → Help and about) to have recordings of your calls deleted from ElevenLabs. Do this before you delete your account, because deleting it also deletes that ID.

If you delete the app without using Delete my account and data, the anonymous account can no longer be reached from your phone and we have no way to link it back to you. We may delete accounts that have been inactive for 12 months.

9. Your rights and choices

You can see and change most of your information directly in the app, and delete all of it with Delete my account and data. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or ask a question about how we handle it, by emailing haydenbanksy@icloud.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You can choose not to use voice calls or the community; the rest of the app works without them. If you are unhappy with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent (for health information, voice calls and the website waitlist) and on the need to provide the service you asked for. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Age limit

Patch is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using the app, contact us and we will delete their information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
