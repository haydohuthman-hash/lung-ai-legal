Lung AI

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026 · Last updated 11 September 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an email address or password. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically.
  • Your quit data is backed up to our server so it isn't lost. Only your account can read it.
  • The community shows other people only your first name and what you choose to post.
  • Voice calls are handled by our voice-AI provider, ElevenLabs. We ask your permission before your first call.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete everything, on your phone and on our server, at any time: Profile → Delete my account and data.

1. Who we are

Lung AI is an app that helps people quit smoking and vaping. It is provided by Hayden Huthman, an individual based in Queensland, Australia (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at haydenbanksy@icloud.com.

This policy explains what information Lung AI collects, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and it also sets out the rights available to people in the European Union and the United Kingdom.

2. What we collect, and why

Information you give us during setup

Your name (only its first word is ever shown to anyone else), your age, what you are quitting (for example cigarettes or vapes), how often and how much you use (as a range), roughly how much you spend each week (as a range), your reasons for quitting, and the date you started. We use this to work out your progress — money saved, time back, health milestones — and to personalise the app.

Your daily logs

Whether a day was smoke-free, any slips (and what they were), cravings you beat, when you checked in, and — if you add them — a mood and a note. We use this for your streak, calendar and progress. Your settings in the app are stored with your profile: sound, the coach personality, style and voice you picked, and whether you have agreed to our terms, to voice calls and to the community rules.

Community content

If you use the community: what you post (up to 280 characters), the optional streak badge you attach to it, likes you give, posts you report, and people you block. Your posts are shown to other users with your first name only. Other people can see how many likes a post has, but never who liked it. Reports are seen only by us, and a post reported by several people is hidden until we review it. Posts and names are checked against a filter for objectionable words before they are published. Blocks are private to you.

Voice calls with Lung AI

If you choose to start a call, the app uses your microphone for the length of the call and sends your voice to ElevenLabs, which turns it into text, generates the coach's replies, and speaks them back. To make the coach relevant to you, we also send your first name, how many days you have been smoke-free, how many cravings you have beaten, and the coach personality, style and voice you picked. ElevenLabs keeps the audio and a transcript of each call in our ElevenLabs account, tagged with your Support ID whenever your account is connected, until they are deleted; you can ask us to delete recordings at any time (see section 8). We ask for your permission before your first call, and you can withdraw it at any time with Profile → Voice calls; we will ask again before your next call. The microphone is never used outside a call you start.

Subscriptions

Lung AI doesn't sell anything yet. If we add a Lung AI Premium subscription, Apple will take the payment: we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app will ask Apple, on your phone, whether your subscription is active, and attach your Support ID to the purchase so it can be matched to your account if you contact us. The count of your free calls each day stays on your phone.

Technical information

An anonymous account ID, created when the app first connects to our server. Your profile and posts are stored under it. The app never asks for an email address or phone number; if you email us, we keep your email with your request. The app shows it as your Support ID in Profile → Help and about. Like any online service, our providers also process technical information such as your IP address and device type in order to deliver the service and keep it secure.

Information that stays on your phone

Messages you exchange with the in-app text coach are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app's features: tracking, progress, the community, and voice calls.
  • To back up your data and restore it if you reinstall on the same account.
  • To keep the community safe: enforcing our rules, reviewing reports, and limiting abuse.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

4. Your health information

Information about your smoking, vaping and cravings may be health information under Australian law, and data concerning health under the GDPR. We collect it only to provide the app to you, with your consent, and we protect it more strictly than anything else we hold: it is readable only by your own account, it is never shown to other users, and it is never used for any other purpose.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run Lung AI, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and live-update service. Your data is stored in Supabase's Sydney, Australia region.
  • ElevenLabs — the voice AI for calls, as described above. ElevenLabs may process and store call data outside Australia, including in the United States.
  • Apple — which distributes the app, and would take any subscription payments. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your use of the App Store and to purchases.
  • Other Lung AI users — who see your community posts and first name, as described above.

When you use the share feature to send something to WhatsApp, Messages, X, Telegram, Reddit or email, you are sending it yourself, and that service's privacy policy applies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's life or safety.

6. Overseas disclosure

Your core data is stored in Australia. Voice calls are processed by ElevenLabs, which may handle that data in the United States or other countries. We choose providers that protect data with measures comparable to Australian law, including encryption in transit.

7. How we protect it

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our database uses row-level security, so your profile and logs can be read only by your own account — not by other users, and not by anyone without your account's session. Your data is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

8. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information until you delete it.

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Profile → Delete my account and data. This permanently deletes your account and everything on our server — profile, daily logs, posts, likes, reports and blocks — and clears the app on your phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete a post: tap the ••• on your post and choose Delete.
  • Call recordings: email us your Support ID (Profile → Help and about) to have recordings of your calls deleted from ElevenLabs. Do this before you delete your account, because deleting it also deletes that ID.

If you delete the app without using Delete my account and data, the anonymous account can no longer be reached from your phone and we have no way to link it back to you. We may delete accounts that have been inactive for 12 months.

9. Your rights and choices

You can see and change most of your information directly in the app, and delete all of it with Delete my account and data. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or ask a question about how we handle it, by emailing haydenbanksy@icloud.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You can choose not to use voice calls or the community; the rest of the app works without them. If you are unhappy with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent (for health information and voice calls) and on the need to provide the service you asked for. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Age limit

Lung AI is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using the app, contact us and we will delete their information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
