Stories are imagined possibilities, not predictions, treatment or promises that an outcome will happen. Story generation needs an available online service; your check-in can remain saved even if a story request fails. Text coaching uses a built-in responder or, when enabled, an online model provided by Anthropic. AI replies are generated automatically. How coaching data is handled is explained in our Privacy Policy, and we ask your permission before your first call.
